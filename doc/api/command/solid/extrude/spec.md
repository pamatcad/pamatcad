# Name

Basic extrusion operation for solids

# Short Description

Extrude operation takes a 2D profile (or co-planar profiles) and extrudes it along a normal direction to create a prism-like solid.

# Description

Select one or more regions of the same sketch or one or more coplanar planar faces.

Start can be defined as a profile plane, offset or object.

There are three extrusion types:

- One side: extrude to length, body, or through all. Taper angle applies.
- Two sides: extrude to length, body, or through all. Each side has its own taper angle.
- Symmetric: extrude to length, with a switch between total length and half-length. Taper angle applies to both sides.

For a to-object extent, advance a planar end cap along the configured side’s extrusion direction. `closest` and `farthest` stop at the nearest and farthest forward points of the selected limiting body, respectively, measured along that direction over the whole body, including points outside the profile footprint. `touch` stops at the first contact between any part of the advancing cap and the body. `intersect` stops at the first position where the entire end cap lies inside or on the body at once. Partial penetration does not satisfy this extent. The extrusion’s Boolean mode controls joining.

Extrusion, by default, affects all bodies on the way of extrusion. Supply `targets` to restrict it to specific bodies.

# Parameters

Lengths and start offsets accept [length formulas](../../../type/Formula/spec.md). Taper angles accept angle formulas. Numeric inputs use mm and degrees.

- `type` is `oneSide` (default), `twoSides`, or `symmetric`. `direction` orients side 1 along the positive (default) or negative profile normal. Side 2 points the opposite way.
- `start` defaults to `{ type: "profilePlane" }`. An offset start takes a signed `offset` in mm along the profile normal. An object start takes a `face` and uses the signed offset from the profile plane to its nearest point, measured along the normal over the whole bounded face.
- `extent1` and `extent2` each take `{ type: "length" }` (default), `{ type: "throughAll" }`, or `{ type: "toObject", body, extent }`. Through all covers the forward reach of the candidate bodies in that side’s direction. A limiting body may be supplied independently of the Boolean targets.
- A length extent uses `length1` or `length2` in mm. `length` is an alias for `length1`. One-sided cuts and joins also accept a signed length when direction is omitted. A symmetric extrusion takes one length with `symmetricLength` set to `total` (default) or `half`.
- `taperAngle1` and `taperAngle2` are in degrees, default to zero, and have magnitude below 90°. A positive angle widens the extrusion away from the start. Symmetric extrusion applies `taperAngle1` to both sides. Only `twoSides` accepts side-2 parameters.
- `mode` defaults to `"newBody"`, which requires positive length. `"cut"` and `"join"` accept signed length.
TK: default mode should be resolved after extrusion

# Preview, results and effects

- `newSolids` - newly created bodies
- `affectedSolids` - existing bodies with changed geometry
- `removedSolids` - bodies consumed by a cut or absorbed by a join

# Topology and references

On a committed extrusion, `feature.faces` selects faces by their origin in one input `profile` (a sketch region or planar face).

| Selector | Faces generated by the extrusion |
| --- | --- |
| `startCap({ profile })` | Cap on the resolved start plane for a one-sided extrusion. The SDK returns `null` for two-sided and symmetric features. |
| `endCap1({ profile })`, `endCap2({ profile })` | End cap for the numbered side. |
| `sideFaces1({ profile })`, `sideFaces2({ profile })` | Surviving faces generated from the profile boundary on the numbered side. Supply `edge` to select descendants of one source sketch edge. |

Side-2 selectors on one-sided features raise `INVALID_PARAMETER`. The original `endCap` and `sideFaces` selectors address side 1.

Each selector follows the faces through the extrusion's own Boolean operation and subsequent cuts and joins. `.all()` returns the distinct surviving face references across the result bodies, including all descendants of a split face and an empty list when none survive. `.only()` returns one face reference, raises `NOT_FOUND` for zero matches, and raises `AMBIGUOUS_FACE` for multiple matches.

Body IDs follow the [body identity rules](../../../../body-identity.md).

# Errors

- `INVALID_PARAMETER` for invalid dimensions or side parameters, invalid target selection, an unreachable extent, or a cut that removes no material.
- `NOT_FOUND` for a missing referenced body, profile, or end cap. `AMBIGUOUS_FACE` if the cap has multiple descendants.
- `KERNEL_FAILURE` if the Boolean fails validation or a cut splits a target.

# Test cases

- Formula length. Extrude a 10 × 10 mm square by `"20/ 2"` in a default scene. Expect a 10 mm cap height and 1000 mm³ volume. The feature retains the expression and its computed length.

- Cube extrusion: extrude a 10 × 10 mm rectangle by 15 mm. Expect one new solid with a volume of 1500 mm³.
- Cube with hole: extrude a 10 × 10 mm rectangle by 10 mm. Sketch a centered circle of diameter 8 mm on the top face. Cut by −5 mm. Expect volume (1000 − 80π) mm³, an empty `newSolids` array, and the original cube as the sole member of `affectedSolids`.
- Multiple profiles, cut with skips: on XY, draw three 10 × 10 mm squares centered at X = −20, 0, and 20 mm, Y = 0. Extrude all three regions by 10 mm in one command. Expect two timeline entries and three cubes. On the left cube’s outward −X face, sketch a centered circle of diameter 5 mm. Preview a −50 mm cut: expect three affected bodies, three timeline entries, and all three committed volumes still 1000 mm³. From the preview results, target only the two outer cubes and commit the cut. Expect four timeline entries, no new solids, both outer volumes (1000 − 62.5π) mm³, and the middle volume 1000 mm³.
- Face extrusion + join extrusion: extrude a 10 × 10 mm rectangle by 10 mm. Expect two timeline entries and a volume of 1000 mm³. Extrude the top face by 5 mm in join mode. Expect three timeline entries and one body with a volume of 1500 mm³.
- Smaller cube on each face: sketch and extrude a 10 mm cube. Enumerate its six faces. On each original face, sketch a centered 2 × 2 mm square and extrude it outward by 2 mm in join mode. Expect 14 timeline entries and one body with a volume of 1048 mm³.
- Cut an adjacent cube. Create two 10 mm cubes, A and B, sharing a face. Cut 10 mm from A’s shared face into B. Expect no new or affected solids and B as the only removed solid. A keeps its ID and volume of 1000 mm³.
- Join adjacent cubes. Create B first, then A, both 10 mm cubes sharing a face. Extrude A’s shared face 1 mm into B in join mode. Expect no new solids, A as the only affected solid, and B as the only removed solid. The result occupies both cubes and has a volume of 2000 mm³. Surviving faces from B resolve on A. For both adjacent-cube cases, preview reports the same body effects as commit while both cubes and the timeline retain their initial state. Commit adds one timeline entry and looking up B returns `NOT_FOUND`.
- To-object extents on a curved body. Set up four independent groups in one scene, translated along X by −40, 0, +40 and +80 mm respectively. In each group’s local coordinates, create a cylinder of radius 15 mm along Y, centered at X = 0, Z = 17 and spanning Y = −10 to 0. On XY at Z = 0, sketch a rectangle from (9, −8) to (12, −2). Extrude it toward +Z with zero taper, in `newBody` mode, using that group’s cylinder as the limiting body.
  - Closest: (−40 mm) expect a flat cap at Z = 2 and a 36 mm³ prism. The cylinder’s lowest point is outside the profile. The bodies are disjoint.
  - Touch: (0 mm) expect a flat cap at Z = 5 and a 90 mm³ prism. Only the cap edge at local X = 9 meets the cylinder. The solids share no volume.
  - Intersect: (+40 mm) expect a flat cap at Z = 8 and a 144 mm³ prism. Its edge at local X = 12 lies on the cylinder and the rest of the cap is inside. At any lower height, part of the cap is still outside.
  - Farthest: (+80 mm) expect a flat cap at Z = 32 and a 576 mm³ prism. The cylinder’s highest point lies outside the profile. The entire cap has passed beyond the cylinder.
  - Check the four cap positions and each feature’s body effects: one new prism, no affected or removed solids. Expect eight bodies in the final scene, with all four original cylinder IDs retained.
- Incomplete cap containment. Extrude a 10 × 10 mm block from Z = 4 to 8 and cut a centered Ø2 mm through-hole. Extrude a centered 6 × 6 mm square from XY toward the block with the `intersect` extent. All four corners can enter material, but the hole remains under the cap. Expect `INVALID_PARAMETER` in preview and commit, with the original body and timeline intact.
- Offset start. From a 10 × 10 mm square on XY, start at offset −3 mm and extrude 8 mm toward +Z. Expect one 800 mm³ body with caps at Z = −3 and +5.
- Parallel face start. Start the same extrusion from a face at Z = +7 and extrude 4 mm toward +Z in `newBody` mode. Expect caps at Z = +7 and +11. The reference body keeps its ID and geometry.
- Tilted face start. Select a bounded planar face whose nearest point is at Z = 2 outside the profile footprint and whose nearest point over the footprint is at Z = 5. Extrude 4 mm from that face toward +Z. Expect flat caps parallel to XY at Z = 2 and 6.
- Unequal sides. Extrude a 10 × 10 mm square 7 mm toward +Z on side 1 and 3 mm toward −Z on side 2. Expect one 1000 mm³ solid, end caps at Z = +7 and −3 respectively, and no start cap or internal face on XY.
- Symmetric total length. From the same square at start offset +3 mm, extrude symmetrically with total length 8 mm. Expect caps at Z = +7 and −1 and a volume of 800 mm³.
- Symmetric half-length. Use the same start and specify half-length 8 mm. Expect caps at Z = +11 and −5 and a volume of 1600 mm³.
- Selected limiting body. Cover the profile with two bodies spanning Z = 3–4 and Z = 8–10. Extrude from XY toward +Z to first touch of the second body. Expect the cap at Z = 8.
- One-sided through-all cut. Place two separated 10 mm cubes ahead of the sketch and a third behind it. Cut a Ø4 mm circle through all toward the first two. Each forward cube loses 40π mm³ and retains its ID; the rear cube is omitted from the result lists.
- Mixed end conditions. From the shared plane between two 10 mm cubes, cut a Ø4 mm circle through all on side 1 and to a planar body limit 4 mm into the opposite cube on side 2. Select only the two cubes as cut targets. Expect volume losses of 40π and 16π mm³ respectively. Preview identifies the same affected cubes as commit and preserves the original volumes and timeline.
- One-sided taper. Extrude a radius-10 mm circle by 4 mm with 45° outward taper. Expect cap radii of 10 and 14 mm.
- Inward taper to object. Extrude a radius-5 mm circle with a −45° taper toward a coaxial radius-2 mm cylinder spanning Z = 1 to 16. With `intersect`, expect a radius-2 mm end cap at Z = 3 and a volume of 39π mm³.
- Independent tapers. Extrude that circle 4 mm on each side, with 45° outward taper on side 1 and zero taper on side 2. Expect end radii of 14 and 10 mm respectively. Querying each side using the same source edge identifies the conical face on side 1 and the cylindrical face on side 2.
- Symmetric taper. Extrude that circle symmetrically with total length 8 mm and 45° outward taper. Expect radius 10 mm at the start plane and radius 14 mm at both end caps.
- Invalid second side. Supplying `length2` to a one-sided extrusion raises `INVALID_PARAMETER` without adding a feature or changing the scene. Requesting a side-2 face selector on a valid one-sided feature also raises `INVALID_PARAMETER`.
